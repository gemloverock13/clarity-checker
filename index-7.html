Does that actually make sense?

The neuroinclusive performance review clarity checker

Performance reviews are full of phrases that feel clear to the person who wrote them but can mean very different things to the person reading them. This is especially true for many neurodivergent employees. Paste in your wording and we'll show you what might need explaining, so your next review conversation is fairer, clearer and more two-way.

Before you start: keep it anonymous

  • Please don't enter real names, diagnoses, health information or confidential performance details.
  • Use general wording, like the text from your appraisal template, rather than notes about a specific person.
  • What you type isn't saved or sent anywhere. It's checked on your own device and disappears when you close the page.
